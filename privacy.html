⚡ MergeIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

MergeIQ reads the pull request diff and repository metadata from GitHub pages you visit. This data is sent to our server solely to generate the PR analysis.

What we don't collect

We do not collect, store, or share your personal information, GitHub credentials, or any data beyond what is required to produce the PR analysis.

Third-party services

PR analysis is powered by the Google Gemini API. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for details.

Contact
